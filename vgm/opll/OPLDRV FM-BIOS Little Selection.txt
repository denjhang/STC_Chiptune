Notes:
I bought this set of tracks from bandcamp for $5, 
and the free accessory has a disk that allows me 
to play the music on the real MSX. But in fact, 
the first four songs can be downloaded for free 
on the closed Hello! KSS♪ website.
https://web.archive.org/web/20011220043211/
suzuka.cool.ne.jp/hellokss/

These tracks do not use the YM2413's 
user tone registers, but instead use 
the YM2413's 15 preset tones. 
The special thing is that naruto uses the technique 
of dynamic switching of timbres, which makes the 
YM2413's own timbres have a different combination.

This set of tracks sounds intimate and enjoyable, 
much better than the mediocre music found 
in many MSX games. 
In fact, there are not many musicians 
who really understand YM2413 composition, 
and I think naruto is one of them.

Track names and order are derived 
from https://naruto2413.bandcamp.com
